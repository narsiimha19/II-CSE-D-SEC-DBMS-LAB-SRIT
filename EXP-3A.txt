# (3a) 1 . Display employee ID, first name and hire date in DD-MON-YYYY format.

```
SELECT employee_id, first_name,
       TO_CHAR(hire_date, 'DD-MON-YYYY') AS hire_date
FROM EMPLOYEE;
```
